What technical UGC actually is

−A generic creator reading your landing page copy on camera.
−Vague praise: "this tool is so easy to use!"
+A developer actually installing, configuring, and using your product on screen.
+Specific, credible detail: setup time, edge cases, real errors, and fixes.
+Content technical audiences actually trust — because it holds up under questions.

Technical UGC is developer-made content — demos, reviews, and tutorials — created by someone who understands the product at the code level, not just the marketing page.

For dev tools and SaaS brands, that distinction is the difference between a video that gets skipped and one that gets bookmarked, shared in Slack, and cited in a comment thread.
